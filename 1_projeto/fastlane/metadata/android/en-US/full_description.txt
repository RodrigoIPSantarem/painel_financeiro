Painel Financeiro is a simple dashboard to follow your monthly investment plan.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Log each investment with one tap ("Já investi") and update your totals.</li>
<li>Set goals and track your progress.</li>
<li>Your data stays on your phone, in a local database.</li>
</ul>

No ads and no tracking. Open-source academic project (GPL-3.0).
